We are seeking a highly skilled and proactive Senior NetSuite Administrator / Business Systems Analyst to manage and optimize our ERP ecosystem. The role will focus on system configuration, reporting, workflow automation, and data integrity, ensuring NetSuite effectively supports operations across consumer goods,” big box” retail partners, and marketplace channels. The successful candidate will also manage and support integrations with key platforms including Workato (iPaaS), Orderful(EDI), Propel (PLM), and Akeneo (PIM) to ensure seamless data flow between systems.

This is a hands-on, cross-functional role that bridges technology and business operations. The ideal candidate will work closely with finance, supply chain, product, and operations teams to analyze business requirements, implement system improvements, and drive automation and operational efficiency.
