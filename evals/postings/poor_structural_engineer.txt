Structural Engineer - Lansing, MI
 
DISHER is partnering with an engineering-forward architecture firm to add a Structural Engineer to their team in Lansing, MI. This company delivers facility-focused projects from concept through construction, combining structural expertise with integrated architectural and engineering design. In this role, you will lead and support structural design for a wide variety of project types, helping ensure safe, efficient, and constructible solutions.
 
What it's like to work here:
 
You’ll join a structural group with deep experience and strong mentorship, embedded in a compact, multidisciplinary firm. The culture emphasizes thoroughness, clear communication, and pride in the quality and clarity of drawings and details. The environment is relationship-focused—both with clients and internally—and is well-suited for someone who enjoys collaborating closely with architects and engineers on complex, high-impact projects.


What you will get to do
- Collaborate with project stakeholders to define the structural scope, objectives, and requirements for facility-related projects.

- Perform structural analysis and design for industrial and manufacturing structures using steel, reinforced concrete, and masonry, ensuring compliance with codes and standards.

- Develop accurate, cost-conscious solutions by applying knowledge of materials, labor, and construction methods to structural systems.

- Review and provide feedback on design drawings and models to verify accuracy, structural integrity, and adherence to project requirements.

- Prepare clear construction specifications outlining materials, methods, and quality standards for structural components.

- Compile coordinated construction document packages, including drawings, specifications, and supporting documentation for issue to contractors.

- Support quality assurance by checking structural designs and documents for compliance with relevant codes, standards, and regulatory requirements.

What will make you successful
- Bachelor’s Degree in Civil Engineering required.

- PE license strongly preferred (effectively required for leading projects); ability to obtain or maintain licensure in multiple states is a plus.

- 5–10 years of structural engineering experience, preferably with facility-related and industrial projects.

- Strong experience with steel and concrete design; exposure to masonry and wood structures is highly valuable.

- Proficiency with Revit; experience with structural analysis tools such as RAM (or similar) is preferred.

- Strong understanding of building codes, ASCE loading requirements, and current steel and concrete codes.

- Proven ability to develop creative, practical structural solutions tailored to different project types.

- Excellent verbal and written communication skills, with the ability to clearly convey structural concepts to both technical and non-technical stakeholders.

- Strong problem-solving skills, close attention to detail, and a commitment to thorough, high-quality drawings and calculations.

- Ability to prioritize tasks in a busy, project-driven environment and function effectively as part of a small, collaborative structural team.
